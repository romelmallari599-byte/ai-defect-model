1Triangle inequality

Two sides of a triangle measure 36 m and 49 m. One possible dimension of the third side is

  • a.84 m
  • b.12 m
  • c.85 m
  • d.13 m

Idea Ang third side ay dapat nasa pagitan ng difference at sum ng dalawang sides.

  1. Rule
    |a-b| < x < a+b
  2. Substitute
    49-36 < x < 49+36
    13 < x < 85
  3. Check choices

    Only 84 is strictly inside. 13 and 85 are not allowed because the triangle would be flat.

a. 84 m
2Area + cosine law

Two sides of a triangle measure 67 m and 50 m. If its area is 1,400 m², find its perimeter.

  • a.191.78 m
  • b.220.28 m
  • c.170.81 m
  • d.229.18 m

Idea Kunin muna ang included angle gamit ang area, tapos cosine law para sa third side.

  1. Area formula
    A=\tfrac12 ab\sin\theta
    1400=\tfrac12(67)(50)\sin\theta \;\Rightarrow\; \sin\theta=0.8358
  2. Two possible angles
    \theta=56.70^\circ \quad\text{or}\quad \theta=123.30^\circ
  3. Cosine law (obtuse)
    c^2=67^2+50^2-2(67)(50)\cos123.30^\circ
    c=103.28
  4. Perimeter
    P=67+50+103.28=220.28
b. 220.28 m

Note The acute angle gives c=57.54 and P=174.54 m, which is not a choice.

3Heron's formula

A highway cuts a corner from a parcel of land. Find the number of acres in the triangular lot ABC with AB = 2025 ft, BC = 1874 ft, and AC = 2877 ft. (1 acre = 43,560 ft²)

  • a.46.84 ft
  • b.43.39 ft
  • c.52.39 acres
  • d.53.45 acres

Idea Alam lahat ng tatlong sides kaya Heron agad.

  1. Semi-perimeter
    s=\frac{2025+1874+2877}{2}=3388
  2. Heron's formula
    A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}
    A=\sqrt{3388(1363)(1514)(511)}=1{,}890{,}136\ \text{ft}^2
  3. Convert to acres
    \frac{1{,}890{,}136}{43{,}560}=43.39\ \text{acres}
b. 43.39 acres

Note Choice b is printed as "43.39 ft". The unit should be acres.

4Area ratio

Triangle ABC has AB = 160 m and AC = 190 m. Point D is on AB, 100 m from A. Point E is on AC. If the area of triangle ADE is 3/5 of the area of triangle ABC, find the distance of E from A.

  • a.142.80 m
  • b.182.40 m
  • c.128.40 m
  • d.148.20 m

Idea Pareho sila ng angle A, kaya ang ratio ng area = ratio ng product ng sides na katabi ng A.

  1. Same angle A
    \frac{A_{ADE}}{A_{ABC}}=\frac{\tfrac12(AD)(AE)\sin A}{\tfrac12(AB)(AC)\sin A}=\frac{AD\cdot AE}{AB\cdot AC}
  2. Substitute
    \frac35=\frac{100\,AE}{160(190)}
  3. Solve
    AE=\frac{3(160)(190)}{5(100)}=182.40
b. 182.40 m

6System of equations

A farmer owns two square lots of unequal size, together containing 6,568 m². If the lots are contiguous, it would require 356 m of fence to enclose them in a single six-sided enclosure. Find the dimension of the smaller lot.

  • a.20 m
  • b.22 m
  • c.24 m
  • d.25 m

Idea Dalawang equation: total area at total fence (6-sided outline).

  1. Let

    x = side of big lot, y = side of small lot.

    x^2+y^2=6568
  2. Fence (6 sides)

    Outline = (x + y) along the bottom, x on the left, y on the right, and the top steps.

    4x+2y=356 \;\Rightarrow\; y=178-2x
  3. Substitute
    x^2+(178-2x)^2=6568
    5x^2-712x+25116=0 \;\Rightarrow\; x=78
  4. Small lot
    y=178-2(78)=22
b. 22 m

Note The other root x=64.4 gives y=49.2, which is not a choice.

7Coordinates

The distances from a point inside a square to its vertices are 3 m, 4 m, and 5 m, consecutively. Find the side of the square.

  • a.5.92 m
  • b.6.07 m
  • c.5.65 m
  • d.5.40 m

Idea Ilagay ang square sa coordinate axes, tapos distance formula.

  1. Set up

    A(0,0),\ B(s,0),\ C(s,s), point P(x,y).

    x^2+y^2=9
    (s-x)^2+y^2=16
    (s-x)^2+(s-y)^2=25
  2. Subtract pairs
    x=\frac{s^2-7}{2s},\qquad y=\frac{s^2-9}{2s}
  3. Put into x² + y² = 9
    s^4-34s^2+65=0
  4. Solve
    s^2=\frac{34+\sqrt{34^2-4(65)}}{2}=31.966
    s=5.65
c. 5.65 m

9Minimum area

A square field measures 2261 yd on each side. A beech tree stands 1898 yd from one corner and 1009 yd from an adjacent corner. A straight fence from one side to an adjacent side must pass through the tree and cut off a triangle of minimum area. Find the area and the fence length.

  • a.A = 971,325 and L = 2,236
  • b.A = 950,160 and L = 2,122
  • c.A = 946,350 and L = 2,495
  • d.A = 939,120 and L = 2,018

Idea Minimum na triangle kapag ang tree ay nasa midpoint ng fence.

  1. Locate the tree

    Corners A(0,0) and B(2261,0).

    x=\frac{1898^2-1009^2+2261^2}{2(2261)}=1702
    y=\sqrt{1898^2-1702^2}=840
  2. Distances from corner B
    p=2261-1702=559,\qquad q=840
  3. Tree is the midpoint

    So the legs are 2p and 2q.

    A=\tfrac12(2p)(2q)=2(559)(840)=939{,}120\ \text{yd}^2
  4. Fence length
    L=2\sqrt{p^2+q^2}=2(1009)=2018\ \text{yd}
d. A = 939,120 yd², L = 2,018 yd

10Quadratic

A 5 m by 12 m rectangular garden plot is to be provided with a footpath of uniform width around its perimeter. If the area of the footpath is 11.20 m², find its width.

  • a.0.56 m
  • b.0.43 m
  • c.0.34 m
  • d.0.65 m

Idea Garden area − natitirang loob = footpath area.

  1. Inner rectangle

    The path runs along the inside edge of the plot.

    (12-2w)(5-2w)=60-11.2=48.8
  2. Expand
    4w^2-34w+11.2=0
  3. Solve
    w=\frac{34-\sqrt{34^2-4(4)(11.2)}}{8}=0.34
c. 0.34 m

Note If the path is outside the plot, w=0.32 m, which is not a choice. The key uses the inside path.

14Unfolding

A room is 40 ft long, 20 ft wide, and 20 ft high. A bug on an end wall, 1 ft from the floor and midway between the side walls, walks to a point on the other end wall 1 ft from the ceiling and midway between the side walls. What is the shortest route along the surfaces?

  • a.40 ft
  • b.60 ft
  • c.58 ft
  • d.62 ft

Idea I-unfold ang room. Ang shortest path ay straight line sa flat net.

  1. Path

    End wall → floor → side wall → ceiling → end wall, laid flat.

  2. Legs of the right triangle
    \text{run}=1+40+1=42
    \text{rise}=10+20+10=40
  3. Straight line
    d=\sqrt{42^2+40^2}=\sqrt{3364}=58
c. 58 ft

Note The obvious route straight over the floor is 1+40+19=60 ft, which is longer.

Quadrilateral ABCD is inscribed in a circle. AB = 90 cm, CD = 70 cm, AD = 50 cm, and AC = 97.29 cm. Find BC.

Quadrilateral ABCD is inscribed in a circle. Angle ADC = 75° and angle ACD = 45°. If arc BC is one-half of arc CD, find angle BCA.

23Tangents

A bridge passes through the center of a circular lake. From point A, 1 km from one end of the bridge (on its line), a road runs tangent to the lake and reaches point B, which is 3 km from C, the other end of the bridge, with BC perpendicular to the bridge. Find the length of the bridge.

  • a.3 km
  • b.5 km
  • c.2 km
  • d.4 km

Idea Dalawang tangent mula sa B ay pantay (BT = BC = 3), tapos Pythagorean sa triangle ACB.

  1. Let d = bridge
    AC=1+d
  2. Tangent from A
    AT^2=1(1+d)\;\Rightarrow\; AT=\sqrt{1+d}
  3. Tangents from B
    BT=BC=3\;\Rightarrow\; AB=\sqrt{1+d}+3
  4. Pythagorean

    Let u=\sqrt{1+d}.

    (u+3)^2=u^4+9 \;\Rightarrow\; u^3-u-6=0 \;\Rightarrow\; u=2
  5. Bridge
    1+d=4\;\Rightarrow\; d=3
a. 3 km

27Segment area
